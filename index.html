About Me

Building Technology With a Business Mindset

I'm Ibrahim Jamiu Kayode (Tech Sheikh) a technology entrepreneur and software developer with a background in Computer Science, focused on building practical digital solutions for businesses, organizations, and people.

I don't see software as just code. I start by understanding the problem, the people it affects, and the outcome the solution needs to achieve. From web applications and backend systems to business websites and digital solutions, I enjoy turning ideas and real-world problems into useful technology.

My technical journey spans software development, Linux, networking, cloud, and DevOps. I'm continuously strengthening these skills through hands-on projects, practical problem-solving, and real-world experience rather than learning for certificates alone.

Through my work and Tech Sheikh Computer Hub, I'm also interested in the bigger picture of technology: helping people develop valuable skills, helping businesses use technology more effectively, and creating solutions that open up opportunities.

I'm still growing as an engineer, but I take that growth seriously. My goal is to build technology that is useful, reliable, and capable of creating real value—while continually expanding the depth of what I can build and solve.
